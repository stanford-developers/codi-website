Our mission

CoDI brings faculty and students together to develop science-grounded methods for coordinating decisions among people, AI agents, or both, build working systems, and test their impact in practice.

Our vision

AI has lowered the cost of building useful systems. The challenge of coordinating decisions remains.

We envision a future in which small teams turn scientific insight and AI capabilities into working systems that coordinate interdependent decisions across people, agents, and institutions, improving outcomes in business and society. CoDI prepares students to lead this work through hands-on development grounded in faculty research.

Our approach

From an idea to a system we can test.

Identify the coordination challenge, build a working system, and evaluate its impact in practice.
