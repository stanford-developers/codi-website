Students

Train students to do the development in research and development.

Student development is central to CoDI’s mission. The aim is to prepare students to turn research ideas into working decision systems—whether they build a business, join an AI-era team, or continue applied research.

  1. 01 →

    Faculty direction

    Frame the question, develop the method, supervise the test, and protect research standards.

  2. 02 →

    Student development

    Combine domain knowledge, implementation, and evaluation to turn methods into working systems.

  3. 03 ↗

    Visible evidence

    Produce a prototype, an experiment, and a paper or project report that makes capability and limitations clear.

The proposed model connects faculty research with substantive development and testing opportunities for student teams, including undergraduates.
